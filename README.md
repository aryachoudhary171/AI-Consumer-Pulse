# AI Consumer Pulse 2026 — What People Actually Want From AI

Interactive AI consumer and product intelligence case study designed for freelance clients, startups, product teams and recruiters.

## Objective
Understand how consumers use AI, what frustrates them, what features they request, why they switch, where trust breaks down and which product opportunities deserve validation.

## Scope
10 representative products across AI assistants, search, coding, writing, creative, education, productivity and career workflows.

## Workflow
Research Question → Source Discovery → Evidence Collection → Data Cleaning → Classification → Analysis → Visualization → Insight → Opportunity → Recommendation.

## Deliverables
- Interactive consumer intelligence dashboard
- Product research explorer
- Category Pulse Index
- Pain-point and feature-demand analysis
- Opportunity Radar
- Need × supply gap matrix
- Switching intelligence
- Consumer persona hypotheses
- Research methodology and audit
- Printable report
- Structured CSV dataset

## Methodology
Primary product/pricing sources are prioritized. Confidence levels, sentiment labels and analyst scores are explicitly separated from verified facts. Analyst indices are directional 1–10 signals and must not be treated as representative survey results.

## Ethics & limitations
No fabricated user counts, market sizes, churn, reviews, willingness-to-pay statistics or geographic adoption rates. Public evidence is selection-biased; prices and features can change. Recommendations require first-party validation.

## Technology
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype for deployment portability. The architecture can be migrated to React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind + Recharts/Plotly for a conventional production build.

## What this demonstrates
Consumer intelligence · Market research · Competitive intelligence · Web research · Evidence verification · Product research · Sentiment classification · Data visualization · Opportunity analysis · Business recommendation.
